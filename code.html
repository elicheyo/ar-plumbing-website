FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How quickly can you respond to plumbing emergencies in the Bay Area?
We offer immediate dispatch 24/7 across the Bay Area. In most situations (Oakland, Berkeley, San Francisco, San Jose, Fremont, and surrounding towns), our technician arrives in 45–90 minutes with fully equipped trucks.
What causes recurring drain clogs and how do you fix them?
Recurring clogs usually stem from invasive tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or cracked sewer piping. We utilize HD sewer camera diagnostic inspection followed by high-velocity hydro jetting to clear pipes to like-new condition.
Do you provide upfront pricing before starting work?
Yes. Armin and the team assess the problem on-site and present a crystal-clear, flat-rate quote. We do not begin work until you give explicit approval. No hidden fees or surprise hourly spikes.
Are you fully licensed and insured in California?
Absolutely. A&R Plumbing and Draining carries full liability insurance and state licensing, giving you total peace of mind for both residential homes and commercial buildings.
